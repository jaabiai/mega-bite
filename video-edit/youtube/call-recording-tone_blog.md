**Facts used (from the screen recording):** Phone app → Menu (three lines, top left) → Settings → General → Call recording →
"Play audio tone instead of disclaimer" → Recording consent → Agree. The consent text: both parties are notified when a
recording starts and ends; the voice notification can be replaced with an audio tone (beep); the user agrees to follow local
recording laws and Google is not responsible. The page notes "Settings take effect only when the feature is available in your
location" and that recording is suspended when storage runs out. The post says plainly that the other person still hears the
beep.
