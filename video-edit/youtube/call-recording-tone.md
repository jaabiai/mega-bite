# YouTube upload pack — V Create: "Record calls without the announcement" (Android, Google Phone app)

Files: `VCreate_Call_Recording_16x9.mp4` (main video, 1:35, no end card) · `VCreate_Call_Recording_9x16.mp4` (Short / Reel,
1:35, the whole talk as one continuous video).

What the setting really does: in the Google Phone app, **Call recording → "Play audio tone instead of disclaimer"**
replaces the spoken "This call is now being recorded" message with a short beep tone. The other person no longer hears
the voice announcement, but they still hear the tone. The copy below says that plainly, so viewers are not misled
(and the video is not flagged for promising secret recording).
