# Reel / Short captions — MEGABYTE: lock your Google search history

Steps as shown in the video: Google app → profile icon (top right) → Manage your Google Account → Data & privacy →
My Activity → Manage My Activity verification → Require extra verification → Save. After that, anyone opening the
search history has to sign in / verify first.
