## Music and sound effects
**100 % original.** Generated by `edit/tools/music_synth.py` (pad, pluck arpeggio, bass, drums, risers, impacts,
whooshes, UI sounds – all synthesised from scratch, nothing sampled, no library, no loops).
Because nobody else owns or has registered this audio, a YouTube Content-ID claim on the music is not possible.
