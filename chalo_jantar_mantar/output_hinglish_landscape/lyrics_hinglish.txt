[Intro]
Ye mitti hamari hai…
Ye desh hamara hai…
Har vote mein chhupa hua,
Kal ka ek sitara hai…
Jab janta ke dil mein sawaal uthe,
Jab sach jawaab maangne chale…
To khamoshi nahin, awaaz bano!
Deshwasiyo… Jaago!

[Beat Begins]

Chalo… Jantar-Mantar…

[Verse 1]
Ungli ki syahi koi rang nahin,
Ye janta ke vishwaas ki pehchaan hai,
Har vote mein kal ki ummeed basi,
Har vote se banta Hindustan hai!
Phir har sawaal ka jawaab mile,
Har shanka ka saaf hisaab mile,
Chunaav ki raah paardarshi ho,
Har naagrik ko vishwaas mile!
Na haq se peechhe hatna hai,
Na sach se nazar churaani hai,
Samvidhaan ki raah pe chalkar,
Awaaz apni uthaani hai!

[Pre-Chorus]
Dil mein sawaal, irade buland,
Haathon mein haq, qadmon mein dam!
Ek nahin, ab awaaz hazaar,
Sun lo janta ki lalkaar!

[Chorus]

Apni awaaz uthaao!
Deshwasiyo Jaago!
Nayi subah ka deep jalaao!
Chalo Jantar-Mantar,

[Verse 2]

Vote Chori! Gaddi Chor!
Goonje sawaal chaaron or!

Ye janta ki pehchaan hai!

Ye apna Hindustan hai!

Na satta se bada samvidhaan,

Milta hai loktantra ko maan!

[Hook]
Chalo Jantar-Mantar!
Jantar-Mantar chalo!

[Verse 3]

Na sach ki awaaz dabe,

Insaaf ka jawaab mile!

Jantar-Mantar ki dharti par,
Ek awaaz bankar aao,
Loktantra ki mazbooti ke liye,
Apne sawaal uthaao!
